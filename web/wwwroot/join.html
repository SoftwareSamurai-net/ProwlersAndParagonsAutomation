How to join the campaign

Everything you need to get your character into the campaign: how to get in, how to join with the code, and how a change you make becomes a change that counts at the table.

You need an invitation first

There is no sign-up. Send your GM the email address you want to use and they will add it to the list. Until it is on there, asking for a sign-in link does nothing at all — and it does not tell you that, so if the mail never arrives, that is usually why.

Five steps, in order

  1. 1

    Sign in — there is no password

    Open Your account, type your address, and a one-click link arrives by mail. Open the link and you are in. Nothing to remember, nothing to reset.

    While you are there, set What to call you — that is the name your GM sees beside your characters.

  2. 2

    Build your character

    Work through the wizard as normal. Your characters follow the account, so you can pick a build up on your phone and finish it on a laptop. Five characters per account.

    If you had already started someone in this browser before signing in, the account page offers to keep them. It never does it silently.

  3. 3

    Join with the code your GM reads out

    The code is ten characters in two halves — Q4TWX-NPRKM — and the hyphen and the capitals are only there to make it easier to say out loud. Type it however you like.

    Campaigns→Games you are in→Join a game→Join

    The character on screen is the one that joins, so open the right one first. If you have not chosen a tier yet, the campaign's tier is filled in for you. If you have chosen a different one, nothing is changed and the page says so — take that to your GM rather than fixing it yourself.

    Campaigns is here.

  4. 4

    Keep building — none of it reaches your GM

    Your rows are yours. Buy things, sell them back, rename the character, start over. Joining a game does not put anything in front of anyone and does not need permission.

  5. 5

    Send for approval when you want it to count

    With the character on screen, press Send for approval on its row under Games you are in. That sends a snapshot; your own copy is unchanged and you can carry on editing.

    Your GM reads a line-by-line diff — Tier: Standard → High Level, Might 6d → 8d, added Flight 4 — and either approves or rejects the whole thing. There is no accepting half of it.

    Only one submission waits at a time. Send again and it replaces the one that was waiting, so if you spot a mistake after sending, just fix it and resend.

What the screens tell you

Where you stand

Approved for Nightfall
What your GM has. This is the sheet to print and play from.
Changes pending
You have sent something and it has not been decided yet. The approved sheet is still the one in play.
Not submitted
In the game, but nothing sent yet. Send when you are ready.

If the code will not take

That is not a code.
A letter or two is off. Check and retype.
No game is using that code.
It has been replaced. Ask for the current one — a new code never removes anyone already in.
That is a lot of codes.
Too many tries too quickly. Wait a minute and try again.
Join is disabled
No character is open. Open one and the button comes back.

Worth knowing

  • A Trait Cap your GM sets is information, not a fence. It shows on your tier page and nothing is checked against it. Going over it is a conversation, not an error.
  • A tier that disagrees with the campaign's is not an error either. Campaigns climb tiers in play. The site reports the difference and changes nothing either way.
  • Nothing is ever repaired for you. An illegal build is reported, never quietly fixed — the numbers stay yours.
  • Sign out and your characters stay put. They live on the account, not in the browser.

Bring the tier, the Hero Point total and anything the diff flagged to session zero, and settle it there rather than over mail.
